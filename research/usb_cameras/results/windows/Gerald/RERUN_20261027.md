## Conditions

- **Light:** the room was dark and the monitor was on. Mean frame luma: C922 about 17 (dropping to about 4 late in the MJPEG run, probably when the monitor dimmed), C930e 56–95 (it faces the lit monitor), generic 15–22 (spikes to 126–132 when the monitor content changed). Earlier daytime runs mostly recorded no luma. Where they did, the C922 was 22–33 and the generic about 15.7, so the generic was never measured in a bright scene.
- **Exposure:** nearly every capture forced manual exposure (2^-7 s, i.e. -7) with AE priority 0, so auto-exposure could not lower fps. Two caveats:
  - The C930e does not reliably hold a manual exposure set before streaming (timing, libusb/Rust): it reads back -5 after capture, and in 4 of 5 valid MF flash runs at -7 it integrated for roughly a full frame period.
  - Concurrent agents changed controls under each other. Prior art saw exposure flip from auto (03:11Z) to manual at the same values (03:35Z). The MJPEG agent left the cameras at manual -7 (C930e -5), WB 4500 K, AE priority 0. Timing's "auto exposure" metadata capture actually ran at the C930e's leftover manual -5.
- **Load:** six rerun agents, then six verifiers, shared the machine and the camera lock (camlock waits up to 472 s). Whole-machine CPU was mostly 2–21%, with brief peaks of 57–60% (bandwidth r05 start, MJPEG msmf C930e 720p). CPU% figures in every area are labelled with start/end machine CPU.
- **Display:** the monitor layout changed since 10-25: the primary is now 1920x1080, and monitor 0 (the flash monitor) moved to x=1920, still 1440x2560 at 59.95 Hz. 1 of 10 timing flash runs (run d) had DXGI refresh glitches and is marked INVALID.

### Kiyo contact

The Kiyo was never streamed and never opened through MF, DirectShow or libusb. It was, however, touched in two ways the reruns first described as "never touched". Its devnode stayed 0x0180200A/0x0180600A with an unchanged arrival time (18:39:51Z) all night, so no harm was seen.

1. **libusb and rawusb enumeration** (naming `libusb_identity.py`, libusb/Rust `libuvc --list`, `py_libusb1 --no-open`, `uvc_iso list` libusb and rawusb). libusb's Windows `init_device()` runs on every device before any `--exclude` filter. It sends hub IOCTLs to the Kiyo's parent port, including a real `GET_DESCRIPTOR(config)` on EP0 (vendored libusb 1.0.27 `windows_winusb.c:1127-1185`; the runs used 1.0.29 and assume it behaves the same). rawusb does the same per-device config read. Naming verifier: **weakened** "never sent a hub IOCTL".
2. **GStreamer ksvideosrc** (prior art: 03:16Z, 03:27Z, 03:37Z). `gst_ks_video_src_open_device` sorts the devices before it matches `device-name`. The sort runs `CreateFile(GENERIC_READ|GENERIC_WRITE)` and a KS property query on every KS capture filter, the Kiyo's included (GStreamer 1.28.8 `gstksvideosrc.c:445`, `ksvideohelpers.c`). Prior-art verifier: **refuted** "never bound or activated". While the Kiyo is excluded, ksvideosrc must be skipped like SDL3, openpnp-capture and `gst --list`.

The paths that really do touch only the listed cameras: MF by symbolic link (mfvideosrc, `mf_mjpeg_capture --dev`, `mf_multi_open --list-match`, `mf_uvc_timing --cam`), FFmpeg dshow by alternative name, pygrabber, cv2_enumerate_cameras, `dshow_exposure_read`, `win_identity_dump --exclude-vidpid`, and `win_usb_topology --exclude` (hub-cached info only).
