CPU % is of the whole machine (28 logical CPUs = 100%). 'before'/'after' = 3 s windows just before the command started and just after it exited. 'background' = summed CPU of every process outside the benchmark's process tree and the wrapper, sampled about once a second while it ran; 'sys - tree' = system CPU minus the tree and the wrapper (includes processes psutil cannot read, e.g. kernel I/O). Idle rule: background mean < 10 and p95 < 10, and system CPU < 10 before and after. Runs shorter than one sample have no 'during' numbers.
