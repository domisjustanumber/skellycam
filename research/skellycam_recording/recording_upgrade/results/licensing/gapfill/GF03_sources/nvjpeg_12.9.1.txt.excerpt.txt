Hardware accelerated JPEG decode is available on the following GPUs architectures - Ampere (A100, A30) Hopper Ada Blackwell Platforms which support hardware accelerated JPEG decode: Windows Linux (x86_64, PowerPC, ARM64) Hardware accelerated JPEG encode is available on Jetson Thor (Blackwell), running Linux ARM64.

[R07] found_verbatim=True
NVJPEG_ENC_BACKEND_DEFAULT Use hardware engine if possible. If not, fallback to CUDA encoder. NVJPEG_ENC_BACKEND_GPU Always use CUDA encoder. NVJPEG_ENC_BACKEND_HARDWARE Always use hardware engine. Do not fallback to CUDA.
